🎮 TO PLAY THE GAME:

  Install dependencies first:
    pip3 install -r requirements.txt

  Option 1 (Recommended):
    ./launch

  Option 2:
    python3 play.py

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BASIC GAME FLOW:

  1. START → Choose to create a new character
  2. CHARACTER CREATION → Pick your class (Hitter, Blaster, or Hider)
  3. JOURNEY → Travel through 5 legs, each with 20 events
     • Fight monsters with multiple tactics
     • Explore dungeons for treasure
     • Level up at the end of each leg
  4. THE CAPITAL → Retire with your gold
     • Buy a house (Cottage → Palace)
     • Or work in a tavern
  5. FINAL SCORE → Your score = remaining gold × house multiplier

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 YOUR FIRST GAME:

  CLASS CHOICE:
    ⚔️ Hitter     → Best for: Combat-focused playstyle
    🔮 Blaster    → Best for: Magic and exploration
    👟 Hider      → Best for: Stealth and sneaking

  DURING JOURNEY:
    When you encounter a monster:
    1. Fight (strongest attacking skill vs their defense)
    2. Sneak (stealth vs their defense × 2)
    3. Steal (stealth + salvaging vs defense × 2)
    4. Stealth Kill (stealth × 2 vs defense × 1.5)

  WHEN YOU FIND DUNGEONS:
    Choose to enter or ignore
    • Defeating the boss grants 200-500 gold
    • 5 floors with mini-encounters
    • Can leave at any time

  AT LEVEL UP:
    Choose 3 skills to boost by +5 each
    • Balance your weaknesses
    • Build towards your strategy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

💡 WINNING STRATEGY:

  Early Game (Legs 1-2):
    • Build up gold (~5,000+)
    • Level up key combat skills
    • Explore all dungeons for treasure

  Mid Game (Legs 3-4):
    • Target 20,000+ gold
    • Equip best gear found
    • Master your combat style

  Late Game (Leg 5):
    • Push for 50,000+ if aiming for Palace
    • Or secure 10,000+ for Castle
    • Every bit of remaining gold matters!

  At Capital:
    • Cottage (1,000 cost) = 1× multiplier
    • Villa (5,000 cost) = 5× multiplier
    • Castle (10,000 cost) = 10× multiplier
    • Mansion (20,000 cost) = 20× multiplier
    • Palace (50,000 cost) = 50× multiplier
