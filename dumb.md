Dumb things that suck
---------------------

The inventory and character screens are two halves of the same coin my inventory should be shown alongside my character stats, put the stats and current equipment on the right, with my inventory on the left.

The buttons to choose a class should action the "start journey" button and start jourey shouldn't be there.

At the end of the fight I don't get to decide to keep or discard the loot, this is wrong.

The invetory doesn't tell me which of it's items is actually equipped so I could make a mistake and re-equip the existing item.

Items in the inventory should be green when they are better than something i own.  For things like armour and weapons and accessories, this should be limited to the type of effect they currently have so that an item equipped for salvaging causes the inventory to highlight items that improve salvaging and fit that slot.  Items that are equal or worse should be red.

The "load game" button should show me the save games we have with the current leg and encounter of the journey and the characters name e.g. "Leg 3 - Event 12 - Hero: Arin" I should be able to then pick one to load and continue from that point.

I shouldn't be able to enter a name for a hero if a save game already exists with that.

A savegame for this run should be deleted if I win and get to the end.  The quit button should always save, so it should be "save and quit" and we should only have one button.

It shouldn't just say "journey events include...", it shouldn't say this at all, it should just show the event that happened.

New change to the game.  The player shouldn't be able to heal at all on the journey.  The player must return to the town at the start of the leg to heal.  Each 10 points of damage is a year of the adventurer's life.  The adventurer starts at 17.  The age of the adventurer is factored into the high score and that's what the pension at the end is for.  The younger the adventurer is at the end, the more cash they need to cover them for a random end age from 60 to 90 and that's what determines their final pension.  We also need a silly blurb text for what the adventurer gets up to at the towns to make money to earn their keep while healing up.  This requires one blurb per year of the adventurer's life spent in town.  The ui flow for this is to show a year of the adventures life while they recovered their points as a "While the adventured recovered from <sillyinjury> on their <bodypart>, they spent they time as a <sillymodifier> <profession name>.".  If they pass the chance to keep the job, then the text will show a follow on sentence that indicates the adventurer was good at the profession and enjoyed the work and the ui will offer a button to "retire as a <professionname>".  It will always offer a "Go back to adventuring" button allowing us to continue the journey with the hp we now have.  When the adventurer gets to age 50, they forcibly return to town and perform the same liquidation as the end of the journey but carry a subtitle of "<heroname> the failed adventurer" on the score board and their score is always reduced to 25% of the value as they abandoned their journey.  There is a 5% random chance that the adventure finds a profitable job in the town in a year and can retire at that point and perform the usual sell off.  This is a choice that is offered to the player.  If they take it the eventual profession is shown as their title like "<heroname> the miniature cobler", this ui is forced so we only have an "ok" button and we then move on to the end screen.  The professions offered should be silly and generated from a real medieval profession and a random selection of humorous modifiers like "miniature", "wandering", "sleepy", or "eccentric", more can be added.
